Our Family Resolution Roadmap

A clear, structured path to achieve stability and a positive post-divorce future.

1

Initial Consultation & Triage

Confidential review of the situation, assessment of immediate needs (e.g., protection orders), and establishment of key goals (custody, finance).

2

Financial & Custody Discovery

Thorough gathering of all financial documents and necessary information to build a clear picture of the marital estate and parental history.

3

Mediation or Litigation

Pursuing resolution through certified mediation or collaborative law; if necessary, preparing and proceeding with aggressive, focused courtroom advocacy.

4

Finalization & Implementation

Finalizing the divorce decree, executing Qualified Domestic Relations Orders (QDROs) for retirement assets, and ensuring compliance with all support/custody orders.
