Our IP Protection & Enforcement Process

A disciplined process for maximizing protection and aggressively enforcing your IP rights.

1

Audit & Strategy

Conduct an IP audit to identify unique assets, assess prior art/use, and define the optimal filing/protection strategy (Patent, Trademark, Copyright, or Trade Secret).

2

Prosecution & Registration

Drafting and prosecuting complex applications (e.g., patent claims) with relevant national and international offices (USPTO, WIPO) to secure rights.

3

Monitoring & Discovery

Continuously monitoring for potential infringement globally; conducting aggressive discovery and investigation when unauthorized use is detected.

4

Enforcement & Litigation

Issuing cease-and-desist letters, initiating federal litigation (injunctions, damages), or pursuing arbitration/mediation for dispute resolution.
