Our Real Estate Resolution Process

A structured approach to strategic dispute resolution and securing property transactions

1

Initial Property & Title Review

Thorough review of deeds, surveys, title history, and agreements to define the legal standing and necessary resolution path.

2

Negotiation & Pre-Trial Motions

Engaging in direct settlement talks or mediation; strategically filing motions (e.g., Summary Judgment) to leverage a favorable position.

3

Litigation or Transaction Closure

Aggressive representation in a property trial, or finalizing all legal requirements for complex acquisitions/sales and developments.

4

Post-Resolution Compliance

Ensuring all judgments, deeds, or regulatory agreements are properly recorded and executed to guarantee a clean, permanent resolution.
